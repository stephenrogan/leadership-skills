# Code of Conduct

This project exists to make leadership work clearer, safer, and more accountable. Contributors are expected to behave accordingly.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct and constructive.
- Challenge ideas without attacking people.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Use synthetic or redacted examples when discussing sensitive work.
- Keep criticism specific enough to improve the repo.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private people, customer, employer, or commercial context.
- Using the project to produce discriminatory, unsafe, or deceptive people-management guidance.
- Pressuring maintainers to bypass the human decision boundaries in the skills.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that weaken safety, privacy, or quality. Serious violations may result in blocked participation.
