# Framework: staff-meeting-designer

## Core job

A meeting agenda that uses group time only where interaction, conflict, or decision-making is required.

## Trigger checklist

- [ ] Weekly staff meeting is due
- [ ] Recurring meeting feels noisy, stale, or status-heavy
- [ ] Important decisions lack a clear forum

## Input checklist

- [ ] Current priorities and metrics
- [ ] Open decisions and blockers
- [ ] Risks, escalations, and cross-functional dependencies
- [ ] Previous action items and owner updates

## Guardrail checklist

- [ ] Do not preserve meetings out of habit
- [ ] Do not bury conflict in status updates
- [ ] Leader decides what deserves live group attention

## Human-owned decisions

The agent should stop at a prepared recommendation when the next step involves employment, compensation, budget, customer commitment, external send, legal/compliance exposure, or sensitive personal information.
