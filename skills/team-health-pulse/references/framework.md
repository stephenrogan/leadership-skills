# Framework: team-health-pulse

## Core job

A grounded team health readout that points to likely system interventions without pretending sentiment is certainty.

## Trigger checklist

- [ ] Morale or energy feels off
- [ ] Attrition, silence, conflict, or missed commitments increase
- [ ] Pulse survey or skip-level feedback arrives

## Input checklist

- [ ] Pulse survey comments and scores
- [ ] 1:1 or skip-level themes
- [ ] Workload and execution signals
- [ ] Meeting participation and conflict patterns

## Guardrail checklist

- [ ] Do not identify anonymous commenters
- [ ] Do not diagnose individuals or private motives
- [ ] Leader validates themes before acting publicly

## Human-owned decisions

The agent should stop at a prepared recommendation when the next step involves employment, compensation, budget, customer commitment, external send, legal/compliance exposure, or sensitive personal information.
