# Framework: premortem-runner

## Core job

A realistic failure map before the failure happens.

## Trigger checklist

- [ ] Initiative is about to launch
- [ ] Plan has optimistic assumptions
- [ ] Failure would be expensive, reputational, or hard to reverse

## Input checklist

- [ ] Plan summary and success criteria
- [ ] Timeline, dependencies, and owners
- [ ] Known risks and assumptions
- [ ] Stakeholder concerns

## Guardrail checklist

- [ ] Do not confuse cynicism with risk management
- [ ] Do not block action because risk exists
- [ ] Leader decides what risk to accept

## Human-owned decisions

The agent should stop at a prepared recommendation when the next step involves employment, compensation, budget, customer commitment, external send, legal/compliance exposure, or sensitive personal information.
