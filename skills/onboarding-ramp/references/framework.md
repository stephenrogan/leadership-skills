# Framework: onboarding-ramp

## Core job

A ramp plan that turns onboarding from calendar chaos into sequenced context, relationships, and outcomes.

## Trigger checklist

- [ ] New hire or internal transfer starts
- [ ] Manager is promoted into new scope
- [ ] Ramp is slow, unclear, or relationship-heavy

## Input checklist

- [ ] Role expectations and success outcomes
- [ ] Required systems, customers, stakeholders, and rituals
- [ ] Known risks, context gaps, and early deliverables
- [ ] Manager check-in cadence

## Guardrail checklist

- [ ] Do not overload the new person with every context source
- [ ] Do not promise career outcomes
- [ ] Manager owns prioritisation, standards, and relationship context

## Human-owned decisions

The agent should stop at a prepared recommendation when the next step involves employment, compensation, budget, customer commitment, external send, legal/compliance exposure, or sensitive personal information.
