# Example: Weekly Leadership Pack

Skill: `weekly-leadership-pack`
Data: synthetic

## Messy input

Metrics are mixed. Pipeline is up, renewal risk is also up. Two managers flagged capacity problems. Product shipped the beta but enablement is behind. The exec meeting needs a short view of what matters this week.

## Generic prompt baseline

> Summarize the week by reviewing metrics, risks, priorities, and next steps. Highlight blockers and decisions needed.

Problem: it says what to summarize, but not what to decide.

## Skill-shaped output

# Weekly Leadership Pack

## Headline

Growth indicators improved, but execution capacity and renewal risk are now the constraint. This week needs tradeoff decisions, not more status reporting.

## What changed

Facts:

- Pipeline is up.
- Renewal risk is up.
- Two managers flagged capacity constraints.
- Product beta shipped.
- Enablement is behind the beta release.

Interpretation:

- The organization may be creating demand faster than it can support adoption.
- Enablement lag could turn a good product shipment into customer confusion.

## Decisions needed this week

1. Do we pause lower-priority internal projects to protect renewal-risk work?
2. Who owns beta enablement catch-up and by what date?
3. Which renewal-risk accounts need executive sponsor coverage this week?

## Risks to watch

| Risk | Signal | Owner | Review point |
|---|---|---|---|
| Renewal slippage | Risk list grows or no mitigation owner | CS lead | Friday |
| Beta adoption confusion | Customer-facing teams lack talk track | Enablement lead | Wednesday |
| Manager overload | Capacity flags repeat without tradeoff | Functional leads | Next staff meeting |

## Recommended focus

Protect renewals and beta enablement before adding new demand-generation work.

## Human decisions required

- Approve tradeoff between internal projects and renewal support
- Assign executive sponsor coverage
- Decide whether beta enablement is now a top-three priority

## Why this is better

The skill output compresses status into decisions, owners, signals, and review points.
