# Source Spine

These skills synthesize recurring leadership and management practices from well-known public management sources and operating patterns. They are not substitutes for HR, legal, finance, or executive judgment.

- Andy Grove, High Output Management: leverage, managerial output, meetings, task-relevant maturity.
- Kim Scott, Radical Candor: feedback that combines care and direct challenge.
- Michael Bungay Stanier, The Coaching Habit: ask before telling, coaching as a manager habit.
- Kerry Patterson et al., Crucial Conversations: psychological safety in high-stakes dialogue.
- Roger Fisher and William Ury, Getting to Yes: interests, options, and neutral conflict framing.
- Michael Watkins, The First 90 Days: stakeholder mapping and transition acceleration.
- Amy Edmondson, The Fearless Organization: psychological safety and team learning.
- Google re:Work: one-on-ones, structured interviewing, manager behaviours, and team effectiveness.
- Atlassian Team Playbook: meeting hygiene, DACI, health monitors, and decision practices.
- Gary Klein, HBR premortem: imagining failure before launch.
- Barbara Minto, The Pyramid Principle: executive communication structure.
- John Kotter, Leading Change: communication, coalition, urgency, and adoption.
- Gallup Q12 and engagement research: recognition, clarity, and manager impact.
- Center for Creative Leadership SBI model: situation, behaviour, impact feedback structure.


## Mega Manager / AI-augmented management references

The mega-manager layer applies leadership workflows to AI-native operating systems: one accountable human managing larger portfolios of work through agents, automation, evals, memory, and governance.

- Anthropic, Building effective agents: prefer simple composable workflows; use agents where autonomy is justified by feedback loops and tool use.
- Agent Skills specification and eval guidance: progressive disclosure, skill packaging, eval-driven iteration, scripts/references/assets.
- NIST AI Risk Management Framework: risk identification, measurement, governance, and management for AI systems.
- Ethan Mollick, Co-Intelligence: humans should treat AI as collaborator while retaining judgment and verification.
- Microsoft Work Trend Index and AI-at-work research: AI changes knowledge-work coordination and management leverage.
- Human-AI teaming research: trust calibration, transparency, handoffs, and accountability matter more than novelty.
- MLOps and LLMOps practices: monitoring, evals, incident response, regression tests, and lifecycle improvement.
